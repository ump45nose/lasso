Lasso includes the following upstream prompt templates. Input placeholders are
adapted to Lasso's selection and question. The translation workflow is adapted
as described below.

Google — Dialogflow CX, Generators / Answering questions
https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/cx/docs/concept/generators#answering_questions
Copyright Google LLC. Documentation licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Raycast — Explain This in Simple Terms - Editable
https://github.com/raycast/ray-so/blob/main/app/(navigation)/prompts/prompts.ts
MIT License. Copyright (c) 2024 Raycast Technologies Ltd.

Fabric — chinese_article_summary
https://github.com/danielmiessler/Fabric/blob/main/data/patterns/chinese_article_summary/system.md
MIT License. Copyright (c) 2012-2024 Scott Chacon and others.

Jim Liu — baoyu-translate
https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-skills/tree/main/skills/baoyu-translate
MIT License. Copyright (c) 2026 Jim Liu.
Adapted into a self-contained translation prompt for Lasso's web and CLI targets.
